Technologia's power, farming and logistics alpha for **Minecraft Java 1.21.1**. Earlier delivered jars and source tags remain available.

Content totals: **31 machine types** (18 in alpha.4), **76 blocks** (40), **68 standalone items** (37), **53 guide articles in twelve chapters** (38 in ten) and **45 advancement milestones** (25). The number of machine tiers is unchanged: eight, Mk I to Mk VIII.

## Power without fuel

Three new generators read the blocks around them and use nothing up. They take tier kits, and a tier multiplies their output like any other generator (1× at Mk I to 12× at Mk VIII). They look at their surroundings once a second.

| Generator | Output at Mk I | What it needs | Buffer at Mk I |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water Wheel | 6 FE/t for each of its four sides with flowing water, 10 FE/t for each side with falling water, up to 40 FE/t | Moving water beside it. Still water (a source block) gives nothing | 20,000 FE |
| Windmill | 4 FE/t at Y 64 or lower, 1 more for every 6 blocks of height, up to 32 FE/t at Y 232. ×1.5 in rain, ×2 in a thunderstorm | A dimension with a sky, open sky above it, and no solid block in the eight positions around it or the nine above it. Connect its cable from below | 20,000 FE |
| Thermoelectric Generator | The hottest side multiplied by the coldest side, among its six sides | One hot block and one cold block touching it. Neither is used up | 40,000 FE |

Thermoelectric values. Hot: lava source 16, flowing lava 12, fire 8, magma block 6, lit campfire 4. Cold: blue ice 3, packed ice 2.5, ice 2, snow block or powder snow 1.5, water 1. Lava and water give 16 FE/t. Lava and blue ice give 48 FE/t.

A generator that cannot work says why on its screen: "Needs flowing water beside it", "Needs open air and sky" or "Needs a hot side and a cold side".

The **Creative Energy Source** is always full and sends up to 1,000,000 FE/t to the blocks beside it and through conduits. It has no recipe and no tier; it is in the creative tab only.

## Wireless power

- The **Wireless Energy Sender** is fed by cable and sends its energy to every loaded **Wireless Energy Receiver** on the same channel, in any dimension. The energy is shared equally between the receivers that still have room.
- Channels run from 0 to 255. Each block has −10, −1, +1 and +10 buttons on its screen. **Channels are public**: they have no owner and no password, so any player can tune a receiver to any channel.
- The sender has a limit button that cycles 100, 500, 2,000, 10,000 and 50,000 FE/t. It starts at 50,000 FE/t.
- A receiver feeds the machines, cells and conduits beside it, up to 50,000 FE/t. It cannot be charged by cable, and it never feeds a sender.
- Both blocks hold 200,000 FE and have no tier, so tier kits do not apply to them. A sender accepts up to 50,000 FE/t from another mod's cable. A Technologia generator or cell still sends at most its own tier's rate into it (200 FE/t at Mk I, 25,600 FE/t at Mk VIII).
- A receiver must be in a loaded chunk. A receiver that is paused, or held back by its redstone setting, takes no power. A sender with no receiver on its channel shows "No receiver on this channel".
- A broken or dismantled sender or receiver keeps its channel on the item.

The **Superconducting Energy Conduit** is the fourth conduit material: 32,000 FE/t per supplier, after copper (200), gold (1,000) and resonite (5,000). One craft makes eight.

## Silver, nickel and new alloys

Silver Ore and Nickel Ore generate in stone and deepslate. Silver: veins of up to 6, five attempts per chunk, between Y −48 and Y 40. Nickel: veins of up to 6, six attempts per chunk, between Y −32 and Y 64. Both need a stone pickaxe or better, drop raw metal, and follow fortune and silk touch. Raw silver and raw nickel crush into two dust and smelt or blast like tin. Both metals can also be sieved with better meshes (see below), which is the way to get them in an existing world.

| Machine | Ingredients | Result | Time and cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alloy Smelter | 1 gold ingot + 1 silver ingot | 2 electrum ingots | 160 ticks at 30 FE/t |
| Alloy Smelter | 2 iron ingots + 1 nickel ingot | 3 invar ingots | 200 ticks at 30 FE/t |
| Alloy Smelter | 1 copper ingot + 1 nickel ingot | 2 constantan ingots | 160 ticks at 30 FE/t |
| Metal Press | 1 invar or electrum ingot | 1 matching plate | 60 ticks at 15 FE/t |

Invar plates build the farming machines, electrum plates the wireless blocks and the superconducting conduit, and constantan the Thermoelectric Generator. The new ores, raw metals, ingots, dusts and plates carry the shared `c:` material tags and the legacy `forge:` tags, like the existing metals.

## Enrichment and infusion

The **Enrichment Chamber** (50,000 FE at Mk I) has one ingredient per lane. Every recipe takes 100 ticks at 20 FE/t:

| Ingredient | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1 coal or 1 charcoal | 1 Enriched Carbon |
| 4 redstone | 1 Enriched Redstone |
| 1 diamond | 1 Enriched Diamond |
| 1 Resonite Crystal | 1 Enriched Resonite |
| 1 glowstone block | 4 glowstone dust |
| 1 quartz block | 4 quartz |

The **Metallurgic Infuser** (80,000 FE at Mk I) has two ingredients per lane, like the Alloy Smelter, in either order. Every recipe takes 160 ticks at 30 FE/t:

| Ingredients | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 2 iron ingots + 1 Enriched Carbon | 2 steel ingots |
| 1 iron ingot + 1 Enriched Redstone | 1 Infused Alloy |
| 1 Infused Alloy + 1 Enriched Diamond | 1 Reinforced Alloy |
| 2 copper ingots + 1 Enriched Resonite | 2 Resonant Alloy |

Reinforced Alloy builds the Chunk Loader. Resonant Alloy builds the wireless blocks, the superconducting conduit, the Resonant Crate and the Resonant Magnet. The Alloy Smelter's steel recipe (1 iron ingot + 2 coal dust) is unchanged.

Both machines, and the Phyto Chamber below, are processors: they gain lanes with their tier, refuse items they cannot use, and are sped up by Resonance Blooms.

## Sieve meshes, the Hand Sieve and compressed blocks

Every sieve starts with a string mesh, which is built in and has no item. Craft a Flint, Iron or Diamond Mesh and use it on an Auto Sieve or a Hand Sieve to install it. The mesh it replaces comes back to you. The Auto Sieve's screen shows the installed mesh. A mesh changes what a block gives; the cycle stays 80 ticks at 15 FE/t.

| Mesh | Gravel | Sand | Coarse dirt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| String (built in) | 1 iron fragment; tin fragment 35% | 1 copper fragment; gold fragment 10% | 1 lead fragment; flint 20% |
| Flint | 2 iron fragments; nickel fragment 30% | 2 copper fragments; silver fragment 25% | 2 lead fragments; tin fragment 50% |
| Iron | 2 iron fragments; redstone fragment 35% | 1 gold fragment; lapis fragment 30% | 2 tin fragments; nickel fragment 40% |
| Diamond | 3 iron fragments; diamond fragment 8% | 2 gold fragments; lapis fragment 50% | 2 silver fragments; redstone fragment 40% |

Meshes are swapped, not stacked: only the installed mesh counts. Five new fragments crush in the Ore Crusher: 4 silver or nickel fragments make 1 dust, 4 redstone fragments make 2 redstone, 4 lapis fragments make 2 lapis lazuli (all 60 ticks at 20 FE/t), and 4 diamond fragments make 1 diamond (120 ticks at 30 FE/t).

The **Hand Sieve** needs no power. Use a block of gravel, sand or coarse dirt on it to fill it, then use the sieve four times. The results drop on top of it and are the same as the Auto Sieve's for the installed mesh. Breaking it drops the unfinished block and the installed mesh.

**Compressed Cobblestone, Compressed Gravel and Compressed Sand** each hold nine blocks: nine in a crafting grid make one, and one unpacks into nine. The Compactor also makes Compressed Cobblestone and Compressed Gravel (100 ticks at 20 FE/t). The Auto Sieve takes a whole compressed block in one cycle of 480 ticks at 15 FE/t, with any mesh: Compressed Gravel gives 9 iron fragments and 3 tin fragments, Compressed Sand gives 9 copper fragments and 1 gold fragment. Compressed Cobblestone is not sieved, and the Hand Sieve takes single blocks only. Compressed Gravel and Compressed Sand do not fall.

## Farming

- **Phyto Chamber** (60,000 FE at Mk I): grows plants from one seed with energy, without soil, light or water. A cycle takes 400 ticks at 20 FE/t.

  | Put in | Get |
  | --- | --- |
  | Wheat seeds | 2 wheat and the seeds back |
  | Beetroot seeds | 2 beetroot and the seeds back |
  | Carrot, potato, sugar cane, cactus, nether wart, sweet berries, kelp or cocoa beans | 3 of the same |
  | Bamboo | 4 bamboo |
  | Melon seeds | 4 melon slices; the seeds back 50% of the time |
  | Pumpkin seeds | 1 pumpkin and the seeds back |
  | Oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak or cherry sapling | 4 matching logs and the sapling back |
  | Resonance Bloom | 2 Resonance Blooms (600 ticks at 30 FE/t) |

- **Auto Harvester** (80,000 FE at Mk I): harvests ripe crops in a square around it, on its own level and one block above and below, and replants them. It handles wheat-like crops, nether wart, cocoa, sweet berries, pumpkins and melons that are attached to a stem, and sugar cane, cactus and bamboo above their base. Results go to its 27 result slots. Its reach is 2 blocks at Mk I (a 5×5 square), and one more every two tiers: 7×7 at Mk III, 9×9 at Mk V, 11×11 at Mk VII. Each harvest costs 40 FE. Like the Survey Miner, it acts for the player who placed it: **that owner must be online and in the same dimension**, otherwise it shows "Owner unavailable". It respects the same block-protection check as the miner.
- **Growth Accelerator** (80,000 FE at Mk I): gives crops, saplings, stems, nether wart, sweet berries, cocoa, sugar cane, cactus and bamboo in the same area extra growth steps, at 15 FE each. At the game's default settings that is roughly seven times the natural rate, on top of normal growth. It spends energy only on a plant that can grow at that moment: not on sugar cane or cactus at their full height, and not on a crop in the dark. It has no slots and needs no owner online.
- **Bonsai Pot**: use a sapling on it to plant it. With no power and no extra space, it grows a small tree in one minute, cuts it and starts again. Each tree gives 2 or 3 logs, usually 1 or 2 sticks, and a sapling one time in five. Bone meal adds a quarter of the growth. The pot holds nine stacks of results; a grown tree waits until its results fit. Use it with an empty hand to take the wood, or put a hopper under it. When there is no wood to take, crouch and use it with an empty hand to take the sapling back.

## Moving and storing things

- **Vacuum Collector** (40,000 FE at Mk I): pulls dropped items within 3 blocks into its 27 result slots, at 4 FE per stack. Each tier adds one block of reach, up to 10 blocks at Mk VIII. Like a player, it leaves an item that cannot be picked up yet, and a display item that can never be picked up.
- **Chunk Loader** (200,000 FE at Mk I): keeps chunks loaded while it has power and is switched on, so machines there keep working when no player is near. Mk I holds its own chunk, Mk III and above 3×3 chunks, Mk VI and above 5×5 chunks. It costs 10 FE/t per chunk at Mk I. It uses the game's own forced chunks, the same mechanism as the `/forceload` command. When it runs out of power, is paused, is held back by redstone or is removed, it lets go of the chunks it forced; when a tier kit or the config changes its area, it swaps the old area for the new one. A loader that holds no area takes one only with 100 ticks of energy in store (1,000 FE at Mk I), and after letting an area go it first shows "Starting up" for five seconds. A weak power supply or a redstone clock therefore cannot make chunks load and unload every few ticks. **A chunk that was already forced by a command or by another mod is left alone**: the loader does not take it over and does not release it. A server can switch the feature off or limit the area (see "Config" below).
- **Crates**: one-block chests that do not join into double chests. Wooden 27 slots, Bronze 36, Steel 45, Resonant 54. Hoppers and comparators treat them like a chest. Their contents drop when they are broken, and a crate renamed in an anvil keeps its name.
- **Vector Plates**: thin floor plates that carry items, mobs and players in the direction of their arrow. The arrow points the way you look when you place it; the wrench turns it. A Fast Vector Plate pushes two and a half times as fast. They need no power, and they need a solid block under them. A crouching player is not moved.

The energy costs above are for Mk I. From Mk III on, a harvest, a growth step, a collected stack and a loaded chunk cost less, in the same way as a processor's operation (down to 70% at Mk VIII).

## Machine controls and the tier band

- **Redstone button**: every machine that can be started and paused has a redstone button on its screen with three settings. "RS: any" ignores redstone, "RS: high" runs only with a redstone signal, "RS: low" runs only without one. A machine stopped this way shows "Paused by redstone". Cells and the Creative Energy Source have no such button. A paused generator or receiver still gives out the energy it already holds.
- **Eject button**: machines with result slots (processors, the Survey Miner, the Auto Harvester and the Vacuum Collector) have an Eject button. When it is on, the machine pushes its results into the inventory directly behind it, opposite its front: up to 8 items every half second at Mk I, and more at tiers with more lanes. The target can be a chest, a crate, a hopper or another machine's ingredient slots.
- **Comparators**: a comparator beside a machine with result slots reads how full those slots are. Beside any other powered machine (generators, cells, wireless blocks, the Growth Accelerator, the Chunk Loader) it reads the stored energy. Beside a Nexus Storage Core it reads how full the core is. Crates and Bonsai Pots give a comparator signal too.
- **Tier band**: a machine above Mk I shows a coloured band at the bottom of its front, in the colour of its tier kit, with one pip per tier number (two pips for Mk II, eight for Mk VIII). Mk I machines and blocks without a tier show no band.
- A dropped or dismantled Auto Sieve keeps its installed mesh on the item, next to tier and stored energy.

## Tools

- **Item Magnet, Reinforced Magnet, Resonant Magnet**: pull dropped items and experience orbs to you from 5, 8 and 12 blocks away. Use the magnet to switch it on or off; it glints while on. It works from anywhere in your inventory. Crouching pauses it, so you can still drop things on purpose, and it leaves an item you have just dropped until it can be picked up. It needs no energy.
- **Travel Staff**: use it while looking at a block up to 32 blocks away to travel there. Grass, flowers, torches, water and other blocks you can walk through do not stop it. It costs one ender pearl from your inventory (nothing in creative mode), needs two free blocks to stand in next to the block you aim at, and has a one-second cooldown.
- **Creative Tier Kit**: use it on a machine to set it to Mk VIII; crouch and use it to set it back to Mk I. It is never used up and has no recipe; it is in the creative tab only.

## Building blocks

Seven more blocks for the factory kit: Floor Panel, Catwalk, Steel Table, Steel Stool, Metal Shelf, Tool Cabinet and Warning Light. The Warning Light gives level-12 light. Metal Shelves and Tool Cabinets face you when placed and turn with the wrench. They are decorative: shelves and cabinets do not store items.

## Guide and advancements

- The Field Guide has **53 articles in twelve chapters**. Power and Farming are new chapters, and there are new articles on meshes, hand sieving, the new metals, enrichment and infusion, ejecting, chunk loading, redstone control and the new tools.
- **45 advancement milestones**: the 25 from alpha.4 plus 20 for the alpha.5 additions. Each is earned by obtaining the item.
- New working sounds for the Enrichment Chamber, the Metallurgic Infuser, the Phyto Chamber and the Water Wheel, and particles on the new machines while they work. `"machineSounds": false` still turns working sounds off.

## Changes to existing behaviour

- **Machine screens have two more buttons.** A redstone button on every machine that can be paused, and an Eject button on every machine with result slots. Both start in the setting that matches alpha.4: redstone ignored, eject off.
- **Comparators read machines.** In alpha.4 a comparator beside a machine gave no signal. It now gives one, as described above. Check redstone builds that have a comparator next to a machine, a cell or a storage core.
- **The Compactor does not make Compressed Sand.** It keeps turning four sand into sandstone. Compressed Sand is made in a crafting grid only. The Compactor does make Compressed Cobblestone and Compressed Gravel from nine blocks.
- **Auto Sieve results depend on the mesh.** With the built-in string mesh the results are those of alpha.4. A better mesh replaces them with the row of the table above.
- **Wireless Receivers charge cells; cells still do not charge each other.** A generator, a Creative Energy Source or a Wireless Receiver charges a cell. A receiver does not feed a sender.
- **The wrench also turns** Vector Plates, Metal Shelves and Tool Cabinets.

## Upgrading from alpha.4

Back up the world before upgrading an alpha.

- **Worlds load as they are.** The saved-data format did not change, no block or item was removed and registry ids are unchanged. Machines keep their contents, energy, tier and settings.
- **Tier bands appear by themselves.** An upgraded machine from an alpha.4 world shows its tier band after its first tick in alpha.5.
- **Config.** An existing `config/technologia.json` keeps working and is not rewritten. The two new keys take their defaults: `chunkLoading` is `true` and `chunkLoaderRadius` is `2`. Add them to the file to change them, then restart.
- **Silver and nickel ore generate in new chunks only.** In an existing world they can also be sieved with a Flint, Iron or Diamond Mesh.
- **Existing Auto Sieves have the string mesh** and give the same results as before.
- **Comparators.** See "Changes to existing behaviour".
- The loader requirements are the same as for alpha.4. The client and the server must both use alpha.5.

## Config

`config/technologia.json` has two new keys. Restart the server after changing them.

| Key | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chunkLoading` | `true` | `false` turns Chunk Loaders off: they let their chunks go and show "Chunk loading is disabled on this server" |
| `chunkLoaderRadius` | `2` | The largest area one Chunk Loader may hold, whatever its tier: `0` its own chunk, `1` 3×3 chunks, `2` 5×5 chunks |

## Install

Choose one jar matching your loader. Use Java 21 and Minecraft **1.21.1** on client and server. Do not install two Technologia loader jars together. The three loader jars are built from the same shared code.

## Known limits

- Wireless channels are public numbers. There is no owner, team or password on a channel, and no screen that lists who uses it.
- The Auto Harvester and the Survey Miner need their owner online in the same dimension. A Chunk Loader does not change that.
- The Chunk Loader uses the game's forced chunks and has no per-player limit and no owner check; a server controls it with the two config keys. A chunk already forced by a command or another mod is left alone. If the server process is killed, not stopped, shortly after a loader let its chunks go, those chunks can stay forced; `/forceload remove` clears them. In a forced chunk machines, hoppers and redstone keep running, but the game itself does not grow crops or spawn mobs there while no player is within about 128 blocks, as with `/forceload`. A Growth Accelerator still works there.
- Eject always goes to the block behind the machine and works with vanilla-style inventories (chests, crates, hoppers, other machines). All faces of a machine still accept permitted inputs and power; there is no per-face configuration.
- The Hand Sieve cannot be filled or emptied by hoppers. Its results drop on top of it.
- The Phyto Chamber grows the plants in its recipe list only; other plants, including other mods' crops, need a datapack recipe. The Bonsai Pot gives a fixed yield of logs, sticks and saplings, not the tree's own drops, and accepts saplings whose log it can find by name.
- Magnets, the Travel Staff and Vector Plates use no energy and have fixed ranges and speeds. There are no Curios slots.
- Tier data is read from the jar at start-up. It is not a datapack file, and the client and server must use the same file. A tier cannot be removed from a machine in survival. Wireless blocks and the Creative Energy Source have no tier.
- The new metals stop at ingots, dusts and plates: there are no storage blocks of silver, nickel or the alloys.
- The Electric Furnace still does not award smelting XP.
- The guide has no hotkey. English is the only language; no translation is included.
- Conduits have no external input buffer. Item Transfer supports vanilla `Container` and sided inventories; capability-only third-party inventories need further adapters.
- Not in alpha.5: fluids, gases, autocrafting, storage drives, server racks, network channels, multiblocks, a multitool, armor, Curios accessories, reactors, dimensions, space, mob farms, linked chests, food and Cooking for Blockheads integration, Create kinetic integration and recipe-viewer integration.
- Draconic and chaotic cores remain creative-only concept items. Decorative sockets and control panels do not provide side configuration or factory controls.

## Verification

Automated server tests run on all three loaders, on headless servers without a game client:

- NeoForge runs the full GameTest suite. It keeps the earlier groups (workshop, processing and power, menus and guide, tiers and lanes) and adds a group for alpha.5: tier bands, the Creative Tier Kit, redstone modes, comparator output, ejecting, the three new generators, the Creative Energy Source, wireless channels, limits and sharing, meshes and the Hand Sieve, the three new processors, the Auto Harvester and its owner rule, the Growth Accelerator, the Vacuum Collector and items that cannot be picked up, the Chunk Loader with its start-up reserve, its wait after a release, chunks forced by someone else and the game's saved list of forced chunks, crates, the Bonsai Pot, Vector Plates, the magnet, the Travel Staff, and the recipes against the content list.
- Fabric and Forge each run loader smoke tests: content, tier data, recipes, advancements and ore generation load; a generator powers a crusher through a conduit; the loader's energy and item APIs follow the machine rules; a dropped machine keeps its tier and energy; the break-permission hook answers; a crate, a Bonsai Pot and a Hand Sieve are created correctly when placed; a Creative Energy Source fills a cell; and a Wireless Energy Sender reaches a receiver on its channel.

The publication workflow builds the three jars, runs the shared unit tests and the resource checks, and requires a passing test summary from each of the three loaders before it publishes. The results for this release are recorded in [VALIDATION.md](https://github.com/Thespiki/Technologia/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

These tests do not replace playing the mod. Nothing in this release is described as checked in a game client. For Fabric, the owner reported a short client launch of alpha.4 that seemed to work; real play testing on Fabric and Forge is still to do. Client behaviour, multiplayer, other mods' cables and pipes, protection mods and modpacks still need to be checked by hand on all three loaders; the [acceptance checklist](https://github.com/Thespiki/Technologia/blob/main/docs/PLAYTEST.md) lists the cases.
