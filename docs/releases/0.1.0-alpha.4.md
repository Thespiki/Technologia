Technologia's machine-tier alpha for **Minecraft Java 1.21.1**. Earlier delivered jars and source tags remain available.

## Machine tiers and tier kits

Every powered machine now has a tier, from Mk I to Mk VIII. Storage cores and terminals have no tier. The tiers are data, read from `technologia/tiers.json` in the jar.

| Tier | Kit | Lanes | Speed | Energy capacity | Energy per operation | Generation | Transfer per tick |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mk I | none | 1 | 1× | 1× | 100% | 1× | 200 FE/t |
| Mk II | Bronze | 1 | 1.5× | 2× | 100% | 1.5× | 400 FE/t |
| Mk III | Steel | 2 | 1.5× | 3× | 95% | 2× | 800 FE/t |
| Mk IV | Resonant | 3 | 2× | 5× | 90% | 3× | 1,600 FE/t |
| Mk V | Hardened | 4 | 2.5× | 8× | 85% | 4× | 3,200 FE/t |
| Mk VI | Tempered | 5 | 3× | 12× | 80% | 6× | 6,400 FE/t |
| Mk VII | Entangled | 6 | 4× | 20× | 75% | 8× | 12,800 FE/t |
| Mk VIII | Stellar | 8 | 5× | 32× | 70% | 12× | 25,600 FE/t |

Lanes apply to processors. Generation applies to generators and solar panels. Faster work draws proportionally more power per tick; the Energy per operation column is the cost of a whole operation compared with Mk I. A higher-tier Survey Miner scans and mines faster and spends less energy per block.

Seven tier kits exist, one per tier above the first. Hold a kit and use it on a placed machine to upgrade it in place. Kits install in order: a Mk III kit needs a Mk II machine. The machine keeps its inventory, energy, facing and settings. Kit recipes:

Crafting an Advanced Energy Cell or an Advanced Solar Generator uses other machines as ingredients. The result keeps the lowest tier among those machines and their combined stored energy, up to its own capacity. Kits on a higher-tier ingredient beyond that lowest tier are not refunded.

## Parallel lanes and input rules

- Processors get 1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 lanes by tier. Each lane has its own ingredient slot (two for the Alloy Smelter) and its own progress line. All lanes work at the same time and share the energy buffer and the result slots.
- Every machine uses one fixed layout: 16 ingredient slots and 27 result slots. Only the slots a tier uses are shown.
- You do not need to sort the input. When a lane is empty and another lane holds at least two operations' worth of ingredients, the machine moves half of that stack to the empty lane.
- A machine refuses items it cannot process. The crusher takes only crushable items, the Electric Furnace only smeltable items, fuel generators only their fuel, and an Alloy Smelter lane only an ingredient that pairs with what the lane holds. A hopper or Item Transfer simply keeps the refused item.
- An unusable item already inside a machine is moved to the result slots, where automation can take it away. If the result slots are full, the machine shows "Unsupported item".
- The Survey Miner's filter slot can only be set by a player, not by a hopper. Only the miner's owner can take its results or change its filter from its screen. Hoppers, Item Transfers and breaking the block are not owner-checked.

## Wrench, conduits and energy limits

- Wrench: use rotates machines, Item Transfers, control panels and ventilation grilles. Crouch-use dismantles a machine into the player's inventory, keeping its tier and stored energy. The machine's contents drop.
- Breaking a machine with a pickaxe also keeps tier and energy on the dropped item. The inventory drops separately.
- Conduit materials: Energy Conduit (copper) 200 FE/t, Gold Energy Conduit 1,000 FE/t, Resonite Energy Conduit 5,000 FE/t, per supplier. The weakest conduit on a run limits what flows through that run; runs that only meet at the supplier are separate and keep their own limit. A block directly beside a supplier is not limited by conduits.
- A supplier sends 200 FE/t at Mk I multiplied by its tier's transfer value. That limit is per tick across everything it feeds, including other mods' cables.

## Auto Sieve, ore fragments and Resonance Blooms

The Auto Sieve is the 18th machine type. A cycle takes 80 ticks at 15 FE/t.

| Input | Result | Second output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gravel | Iron Ore Fragment | Tin Ore Fragment, 35% |
| Sand | Copper Ore Fragment | Gold Ore Fragment, 10% |
| Coarse dirt | Lead Ore Fragment | Flint, 20% |

Four matching fragments crush into one dust. The crusher also turns cobblestone into gravel and gravel into sand. Processing recipes accept an optional `byproduct_chance` between 0 and 1.

The Resonance Bloom is a plant crafted from a Resonite Crystal, any small flower and glowstone dust. It is planted on soil. Each bloom within two blocks of a processing machine, in any direction, adds 8% speed, up to five blooms and 40%. The energy per operation does not change.

## Guide and machine feedback

- The Field Guide has **38 articles in ten chapters**, with a new Upgrades chapter. A Progress page shows the workshop advancements as a route with done, next and locked states. It shows the advancements the game has revealed to the player so far, so it fills in as the player advances.
- **25 advancement milestones**: the 20 from alpha.3 plus the Auto Sieve, the first tier kit, the Resonant tier kit, the gold conduit and the Resonance Bloom. Each is earned by obtaining the item.
- Working processors and fuel generators emit particles and play a quiet working sound; the Survey Miner emits particles. `"machineSounds": false` in `config/technologia.json` turns the working sounds off. Machines, cables and building blocks use metal sounds; reinforced glass uses glass sounds. Every machine type has its own model.
- Akashic Tome: the Field Guide's item id (`technologia:field_guide`) contains "guide", so the NeoForge 1.21.1 build of Akashic Tome should accept it with its default settings. This was read from that mod's source and has not been tested in game. No Forge or Fabric build of Akashic Tome for 1.21.1 was found.

Content totals: 40 blocks (36 in alpha.3, plus the Auto Sieve, two conduits and the Resonance Bloom) and 37 standalone items (25 in alpha.3, plus five fragments and seven tier kits).

## Fixes

- Generators no longer burn fuel when the buffer cannot take a full tick of output.
- The Survey Miner's status no longer goes stale. Start after a completed scan begins a new scan.
- Each of the ten building blocks has a distinct recipe. In alpha.3 several of them shared the same ingredients.
- Control panels and ventilation grilles face the player when placed and turn with the wrench.
- The Item Transfer outline matches its model.
- Network Cable, Energy Conduits and Item Transfers drop when broken by hand. Machines and building blocks need a pickaxe of any tier; alpha.3 asked for a stone pickaxe or better.
- Tin and lead dust give the same smelting experience as the other dusts.
- A partial config file keeps the defaults for its missing keys.

## Performance and packaging

- Conduit routes and storage network walks are cached and rebuilt only when blocks change, with a periodic refresh.
- Processors reuse the last matching recipe.
- The terminal catalogue is rebuilt only when its contents, search text or order change.
- Item Transfers no longer all fire on the same tick.
- Item Transfers extract by hopper rules: on any side of a vanilla furnace they take the smelted items, not the fuel or the ingredient. They also fill blocks that accept one item per slot, such as the chiseled bookshelf.
- The NeoForge GameTests moved to `neoforge/src/gametest` and are no longer in the NeoForge jar. Fabric and Forge gained their own test source sets, which are not packaged either.
- Archives are built without timestamps, for reproducibility.
- Every jar carries the project licence, the template licences and a third-party notice.
- Loader version ranges in the metadata come from `gradle.properties`.

## Upgrading from alpha.3

Back up the world before upgrading an alpha.

- **Worlds migrate automatically.** Machine data now carries a format marker. Alpha.3 machines have no marker and are converted when they load: they keep their contents and stored energy, ingredients stay in the ingredient slots, and results move to the new result slots. Storage cores keep their 54 slots. Existing machines load as Mk I. An unfinished processing cycle restarts; its input is not consumed by the restart.
- **Nothing is removed from a world.** No block or item was removed and registry ids are unchanged. Alpha.3 had no separate energy export block. Its code contained an internal energy-export hook that nothing called, and alpha.4 removes that hook. Power leaves a generator or cell the same way as before: it pushes to the blocks directly beside it and through Energy Conduits, including other mods' blocks that accept FE (Forge, NeoForge) or Team Reborn Energy (Fabric), and another mod's cable can pull from it through the same energy adapter.
- **Energy limits are per tick.** A machine's transfer limit used to apply to each call from another mod's cable. It now applies per tick and is shared with conduit routing. A Mk I machine sends at most 200 FE in one tick and accepts at most 200 FE per tick from another mod's cable, so check pack setups that moved more than that.
- **Machines refuse unusable items.** Hoppers and pipes that pushed unusable items into machines will now keep those items. A hopper can no longer place the Survey Miner's filter.
- **The wrench's crouch-use changed.** In alpha.3 it showed a machine's stored energy. It now dismantles the machine. Stored energy is shown when hovering the energy bar in the machine screen and on the machine's item tooltip.
- **Dropped machines keep their energy.** In alpha.3 a broken machine lost its stored energy. It now keeps tier and energy on the item.
- **Config.** An existing `config/technologia.json` keeps working and is not rewritten. To turn working sounds off, add `"machineSounds": false` to it and restart.
- **Fabric API minimum.** The Fabric jar now asks for Fabric API 0.109.0+1.21.1 or newer, the version it is built and tested against. Alpha.3 accepted any Fabric API version.
- Existing ore generation still affects only newly generated chunks.

## Install

Choose one jar matching your loader. Use Java 21 and Minecraft **1.21.1** on client and server. Do not install two Technologia loader jars together. The three loader jars are built from the same shared code.

| Loader | Jar | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fabric | `technologia-fabric-1.21.1-0.1.0-alpha.4.jar` | Fabric Loader 0.16.9+ and Fabric API 0.109.0+1.21.1 or newer; Team Reborn Energy bundled |
| NeoForge | `technologia-neoforge-1.21.1-0.1.0-alpha.4.jar` | NeoForge 21.1.80+ |

## Known limits

- Tier data is read from the jar at start-up. It is not a datapack file, and the client and server must use the same file.
- Machine tiers do not change a machine's model yet. A tier cannot be removed from a machine once installed.
- The Electric Furnace still does not award smelting XP.
- All faces of a machine accept permitted inputs and power; there is no per-face configuration.
- The guide has no hotkey. English is the only language; no translation is included.
- The Progress page lists only the advancements the game has revealed to the player. It has no rewards and no team progress.
- Conduits have no external input buffer. Item Transfer supports vanilla `Container` and sided inventories; capability-only third-party inventories need further adapters.
- Not in alpha.4: fluids, gases, autocrafting, storage disks, server racks, network channels, multiblocks, tools and armor, crops, dimensions, Create kinetic integration and recipe-viewer integration.
- Planned for later, together with the systems they depend on, and not started: rack cooling (needs server racks and network power), mob essence (needs mob farms), an orbital solar relay (needs space and wireless power) and data-cable materials (needs the channel system).
- Draconic and chaotic cores remain creative-only concept items. Decorative sockets and control panels do not provide side configuration or factory controls.

## Verification

Automated server tests run on all three loaders, on headless servers without a game client:

- NeoForge runs the full GameTest suite, which covers processing, power and conduits, item transfer, storage, the machine and storage menus, mining, tiers, lanes, input refusal, drops, the alpha.3 save migration and the other alpha.4 additions.
- Fabric and Forge each run loader smoke tests: content, tier data, recipes, advancements and ore generation load; a generator powers a crusher through a conduit; the loader's energy and item APIs follow the machine rules; a dropped machine keeps its tier and energy; and the break-permission hook answers.

The publication workflow builds the three jars, runs the shared unit tests and the resource checks, and requires a passing test summary from each of the three loaders before it publishes. The results for this release are recorded in [VALIDATION.md](https://github.com/Thespiki/Technologia/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

These tests do not replace playing the mod. Client behaviour on Fabric and Forge, multiplayer, other mods' cables and pipes, protection mods and modpacks still need to be checked by hand; the [acceptance checklist](https://github.com/Thespiki/Technologia/blob/main/docs/PLAYTEST.md) lists the cases.
