# Alpha.5 acceptance checklist

Record Minecraft, Java, loader, modpack and Technologia versions with each result. These are cases for a person to play through in the game. They are acceptance cases, not completed-test claims. Put results and remaining gaps in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md). Run on Fabric, Forge and NeoForge independently, on clients and dedicated servers.

The first section lists the cases added for alpha.5 and the second those added for alpha.4. The later sections are the earlier checklist, kept and brought up to date where a later version changed the behavior. Exact numbers and recipes are in the [alpha.5 release notes](releases/0.1.0-alpha.5.md).

## Alpha.5 additions

1. **Tier band.** Place a Mk I machine: its front shows no band. Install a Bronze Tier Kit: a band in the kit's colour appears at the bottom of the front with two pips. Walk the machine up to Mk VIII and check that the colour changes with each kit and that the pips go from two to eight. Check the band on all four facings and after turning the machine with the wrench. Dismantle a Mk IV machine and place it again: the band must be there at once. Check that a Wireless Energy Sender, a Creative Energy Source, a storage core and a terminal never show a band.
2. **Redstone control.** Open a crusher with ingredients and power. The redstone button reads "RS: any" and the machine works. Press it once ("RS: high"): the machine stops and shows "Paused by redstone"; put a lever on it and switch it on: it works again. Press the button again ("RS: low"): it works only while the lever is off. Hovering the button shows its explanation. Repeat on a generator, the Survey Miner, a Wireless Energy Receiver and a Chunk Loader. Energy cells and the Creative Energy Source must have no redstone button. A generator paused this way must still give out the energy it already holds. Save, reload and check that the setting is kept.
3. **Comparator output.** Put a comparator against a processor: the signal must be 0 with empty result slots, rise as they fill, and reach 15 when all 27 hold full stacks. Put one against an energy cell: 0 when empty, 15 when full. Check a generator, a Growth Accelerator and a storage core (which reads how full the core is). The signal must update when items are taken out and when the machine is broken.
4. **Result ejection.** Put a chest directly behind a crusher (the side opposite its front). Switch Eject on: results must move into the chest, about eight items every half second at Mk I, and the result slots must empty. With Eject off nothing moves. Replace the chest with a crate, a hopper and another machine: results that the second machine can process must enter its ingredient slots, and others must stay. With no inventory behind the machine nothing is lost. Turn the machine with the wrench and check that "behind" follows the new facing. Check the Eject button on the Survey Miner, the Auto Harvester and the Vacuum Collector, and that machines without result slots have no Eject button.
5. **Water Wheel.** Place a Water Wheel with a water source block against one side: it must show "Needs flowing water beside it". Let water flow past one side: the energy tooltip must show 6 FE/t. Flowing water on all four sides: 24 FE/t. Falling water on one side: 10 FE/t; on all four: 40 FE/t. Install tier kits and check that the output is multiplied (1.5× at Mk II, 12× at Mk VIII). It must need no fuel and have no slots. Changes to the water must show within about a second.
6. **Windmill.** Place a Windmill in the open at Y 64 with a conduit coming from below: 4 FE/t. Rebuild it at Y 70 (5 FE/t), Y 100 (10 FE/t) and Y 232 or higher (32 FE/t). Start rain (×1.5) and a thunderstorm (×2). Put a solid block beside it, diagonally beside it, and in each of the nine positions above it: each must stop it with "Needs open air and sky". A roof higher up must stop it too. Check that a conduit attached to its side stops it and that one attached below does not. It must not work in the Nether or the End.
7. **Thermoelectric Generator.** Place one with lava on one side and water on another: 16 FE/t. Replace the water with blue ice: 48 FE/t. Check the other values from the guide (flowing lava 12, fire 8, magma block 6, lit campfire 4; packed ice 2.5, ice 2, snow block or powder snow 1.5, water 1). With only a hot side or only a cold side it must show "Needs a hot side and a cold side". Run it for several minutes and check that the generator uses up neither the lava nor the packed or blue ice.
8. **Creative Energy Source and Creative Tier Kit.** Both must be in the creative tab and have no recipe. The source must always be full and fill a cell beside it. Use the Creative Tier Kit on a Mk I machine: it becomes Mk VIII and the kit is kept. Crouch and use it: back to Mk I, with the message on screen. Use it on a wireless block and a storage core: the message says the block has no tiers.
9. **Wireless power.** Feed a Wireless Energy Sender from a cell. Place a Wireless Energy Receiver with a machine beside it. With different channels the sender shows "No receiver on this channel" and nothing arrives. Set the same channel with the −10, −1, +1 and +10 buttons (check that 255 + 1 wraps to 0): the machine receives power. Cycle the sender's limit through 100, 500, 2,000, 10,000 and 50,000 FE/t and compare what arrives. Add a second receiver on the channel: the power must be shared about equally. Put a receiver in the Nether and keep its chunk loaded with a second player or a Chunk Loader: it must receive. Try to charge a receiver with a conduit: it must refuse. Pause a receiver: it must take no more power. Break a sender and place it again: it keeps its channel. With a second player, confirm that anyone can tune a receiver to the channel: channels are public.
10. **Superconducting conduit.** Check the tooltip: 32,000 FE/t per supplier. Feed consumers from a Mk VIII cell (25,600 FE/t) through superconducting conduits: the full rate must arrive. Add one copper segment to the run: it must fall to 200 FE/t.
11. **Silver, nickel and alloys.** In newly generated chunks, find Silver Ore (Y −48 to 40) and Nickel Ore (Y −32 to 64), in stone and deepslate. Mine them with a wooden pickaxe (no drop), a stone pickaxe (raw metal), fortune and silk touch. Crush raw silver and raw nickel into two dust; smelt and blast the ore, the raw metal and the dust into ingots. In the Alloy Smelter make electrum (gold + silver → 2), invar (2 iron + nickel → 3) and constantan (copper + nickel → 2), in both slot orders. Press invar and electrum into plates. Check that the Survey Miner mines the new ores.
12. **Enrichment Chamber.** Enrich coal, charcoal, four redstone, a diamond and a Resonite Crystal: one enriched item each, 100 ticks at 20 FE/t at Mk I. Three redstone must wait for a fourth. A glowstone block gives four dust and a quartz block four quartz. Unusable items must be refused. Check lanes at Mk III and above, and the bloom boost.
13. **Metallurgic Infuser.** Make steel (2 iron + Enriched Carbon → 2), Infused Alloy (iron + Enriched Redstone), Reinforced Alloy (Infused Alloy + Enriched Diamond) and Resonant Alloy (2 copper + Enriched Resonite → 2), each in both slot orders, 160 ticks at 30 FE/t at Mk I. A lane holding iron must refuse an ingredient that does not pair with it. Feed it from two hoppers.
14. **Sieve meshes.** An Auto Sieve's screen must read "Mesh: String". Use a Flint Mesh on it: the message says it is installed, one mesh is used, and the screen reads "Mesh: Flint". Use an Iron Mesh: the Flint Mesh comes back. Use the same mesh again: "That mesh is already installed" and nothing is used. For each mesh, sieve gravel, sand and coarse dirt and compare with the table in the release notes; over at least 200 cycles the second output should appear about as often as listed (for example diamond fragments from gravel about 8% of the time with the Diamond Mesh). Crush four silver, nickel, redstone, lapis and diamond fragments. Dismantle the sieve and place it again: it keeps its mesh.
15. **Hand Sieve.** Use a block of gravel on an empty Hand Sieve: the tray fills. Use the sieve four times: the layer sinks each time and the results drop on top after the fourth. Using it empty shows "Put gravel, sand or coarse dirt in the sieve first". A block it cannot sieve, and a compressed block, show "The sieve cannot use that" and stay in your hand; the block must not be placed against the sieve either. With `/gamerule doTileDrops false` the results must still drop. Install each mesh and compare the results with the Auto Sieve's. Break a half-used sieve: the block and the mesh must drop. It needs no power, and a hopper must not be able to fill or empty it.
16. **Compressed blocks.** Craft nine cobblestone, gravel and sand into one compressed block each, and each back into nine. Make Compressed Cobblestone and Compressed Gravel in the Compactor; sand in the Compactor must still give sandstone and never Compressed Sand. Sieve Compressed Gravel (9 iron and 3 tin fragments, 480 ticks at Mk I) and Compressed Sand (9 copper and 1 gold fragment) with each mesh: the result must not change with the mesh. Place Compressed Gravel and Compressed Sand over air: they must not fall.
17. **Phyto Chamber.** Grow each entry of the table in the release notes from one item: 400 ticks at 20 FE/t at Mk I (600 ticks at 30 FE/t for a Resonance Bloom). Check that the seed or sapling comes back where listed, that melon seeds come back about half the time, and that the chamber stops with "Output is full" without losing the seed. Items that are not in the list must be refused.
18. **Auto Harvester.** Place one beside a field with wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart, cocoa, a sweet berry bush, melon and pumpkin stems with fruit, sugar cane, cactus and bamboo. Ripe plants must be harvested into the result slots and replanted; unripe plants, the stems, the bottom block of cane, cactus and bamboo, and a pumpkin placed by hand with no stem must be left. Check the area: 5×5 at Mk I, 7×7 at Mk III, 9×9 at Mk V, 11×11 at Mk VII, on its own level and one block above and below. Each harvest costs 40 FE at Mk I. Log the owner out or send them to another dimension: it must stop with "Owner unavailable". Fill its result slots: "Output is full", and nothing is lost. Check that it does not harvest in a protected area.
19. **Growth Accelerator.** Plant two equal fields and put a powered Growth Accelerator beside one. That field must ripen clearly sooner. Without power it must do nothing and show "Needs power". With nothing to grow in reach it must go back to "Ready" and use no energy; that includes sugar cane and cactus at their full height. Check saplings, stems, sugar cane, cactus and bamboo. It has no slots and no owner rule.
20. **Vacuum Collector.** Drop items 2, 3 and 5 blocks from a Mk I collector: the first two must be pulled into its result slots, the third left. Check above and below it. Upgrade it and check one more block of reach per tier. With full result slots it must show "Output is full" and leave the items on the ground. Pause it and check that it stops collecting. Throw an item next to it: it must be left for about two seconds, as a player would have to wait. An item summoned with `PickupDelay:32767` must never be taken. Combine it with an Auto Harvester and with Eject.
21. **Chunk Loader.** Power a Chunk Loader beside a working machine, travel far beyond the simulation distance, come back and check that the machine kept working. A Mk I loader holds its own chunk; Mk III 3×3; Mk VI 5×5 (check with `/forceload query`). It must use 10 FE/t per chunk at Mk I. An empty loader must show "Needs power" until it holds 100 ticks of energy (1,000 FE at Mk I). Cut its power, pause it, hold it back with redstone and break it: each time `/forceload query` must show its chunks released. Started again, it must show "Starting up" for five seconds before it forces its chunks again. Feed a Mk VI loader from a single Water Wheel: it must load its area for a while and let it go for a while, not switch every few ticks. Put a Mk III tier kit on a running Mk II loader: the area must grow without a pause. Force a chunk with `/forceload add` before placing a loader in it, then remove the loader: the chunk must stay forced. Place two loaders with overlapping areas and remove one: the shared chunks must stay loaded. Set `"chunkLoading": false` and restart: the loader shows "Chunk loading is disabled on this server". Set `"chunkLoaderRadius"` to `0` or `1`, restart, and check the area of a Mk VI loader. Restart the server with a running loader and check that its chunks are loaded again. While a loader runs, force another chunk with `/forceload add`, wait a minute, stop the server and start it again: that chunk must still be forced. With no player near, check what does and does not happen in the loaded chunks: machines must run; crops are not expected to grow by themselves; an Auto Harvester still needs its owner online in that dimension.
22. **Crates.** Craft the four crates in order. Check 27, 36, 45 and 54 slots, that two crates side by side stay separate, that hoppers fill and empty them, that a comparator reads them and that the contents drop when a crate is broken. Rename a crate in an anvil, place it and break it: the item must keep the name. The Wooden Crate breaks fastest with an axe, the others with a pickaxe.
23. **Bonsai Pot.** Use a sapling on a pot: one sapling is used and a sprout shows. Use it with an empty hand: it shows "Growing" with a percentage. After about a minute the tree is full size, is cut, and starts again. Take the wood with an empty hand: two or three logs each time, often sticks, sometimes a sapling. Use bone meal: growth jumps by a quarter. Put a hopper under the pot: it must take the wood and never the planted sapling. Leave the pot until its nine result slots are full: the grown tree must wait. Crouch and use it with an empty hand when it holds no wood: the sapling comes back. Try every vanilla sapling, a mangrove propagule, an azalea and both nether fungi. Check the four growth stages and the comparator signal.
24. **Vector Plates.** Place a plate: its arrow points the way you were looking. Drop an item on it, walk onto it and lead a mob onto it: each is carried along the arrow. Crouch on it: you are not moved. Build a line with a corner. Compare a Fast Vector Plate. Turn a plate with the wrench. Remove the block under a plate: the plate must drop as an item.
25. **Magnets.** Hold an Item Magnet and use it: "Magnet on", and it glints. Dropped items and experience orbs within 5 blocks must come to you; an item you have just thrown must stay down for a moment before it returns. Crouch: nothing is pulled. Use it again: "Magnet off". Keep it in an inventory slot rather than in your hand: it must still work. Check 8 blocks for the Reinforced Magnet and 12 for the Resonant Magnet, and the tooltip of each.
26. **Travel Staff.** With an ender pearl in the inventory, use the staff while looking at a block up to 32 blocks away: you arrive beside or on top of it, one pearl is used, and you take no fall damage from the trip. Look at the sky or at a block farther away: "No block in reach to travel to". Without a pearl: the message asks for one. Aim at a block with no room to stand: no travel and no pearl used. Aim at a distant block across grass, flowers or shallow water, and while standing in tall grass: the staff must go to the block and not stop at the plant. In creative mode no pearl is needed. Check the one-second cooldown.
27. **New building blocks.** Craft and place the Floor Panel, Catwalk, Steel Table, Steel Stool, Metal Shelf, Tool Cabinet and Warning Light. Check their models from every side, that the outline matches the model, that the Catwalk is see-through, that the Warning Light gives level-12 light, and that shelves and cabinets face you when placed and turn with the wrench. They must not open an inventory.
28. **Guide and advancements.** Read the twelve chapters and 53 articles; Power and Farming are new chapters. Check that each number in the new articles matches what you measured in the cases above. Obtain each of the 20 new milestone items and check the Progress page and the vanilla Advancements screen for all 45.
29. **Alpha.4 world upgrade.** Back up an alpha.4 world with machines at several tiers, items in their slots, charged cells, a running miner and a four-core Nexus. Load it in alpha.5 with the alpha.4 `config/technologia.json` left in place. There must be no missing-registry warnings. Every machine keeps its tier, energy, contents and settings; item counts are unchanged; upgraded machines show their tier band within a moment of loading; Auto Sieves read "Mesh: String" and give the alpha.4 results; redstone buttons read "RS: any" and Eject is off. The config file must not be rewritten, and Chunk Loaders must work with the defaults.
30. **Fabric and Forge client check.** The automated tests for these loaders run on a headless server only. On each loader, start a client and a dedicated server with only Technologia (plus Fabric API on Fabric). Check the log for errors, that all 76 blocks and 68 standalone items are present and textured, that the Catwalk, Bonsai Pot, Hand Sieve, Reinforced Glass and Resonance Bloom are see-through where they should be, that silver and nickel ore generate, and that the machine screen, guide and Progress page open. Then run cases 1, 4, 9, 18 and 21 on that loader. Energy uses FE on Forge and Team Reborn Energy on Fabric.

## Alpha.4 additions

These cases are kept as written for alpha.4. Where a count has grown since (machine types, blocks, items, articles, milestones), use the alpha.5 numbers from the section above.

1. **Tier kit install and order.** Craft all seven tier kits from their recipes. Use a Bronze Tier Kit on a Mk I machine: it becomes Mk II, one kit is consumed, and inventory, energy, facing and settings are unchanged. Use a Steel kit on a Mk I machine: it must be refused with the message that the machine must be Mk II first, and the kit is kept. Use a kit of the same or a lower tier: refused, kit kept. Use a kit on a storage core and a terminal: refused, because these blocks have no tier. Walk one machine from Mk I to Mk VIII and check the tier name at the top right of its screen, capacity (1× to 32×) and transfer rate (200 to 25,600 FE/t) at each step. Only the owner may upgrade a Survey Miner.
2. **Lanes and auto-spread.** Check that a processor shows 1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 ingredient slots from Mk I to Mk VIII, and an Alloy Smelter twice as many (two per lane). Each lane must have its own progress line and all lanes must run at the same time. Put one large stack in the first lane of a Mk III or higher machine: within about a second an empty lane must receive about half of it, always a whole number of operations (27 ingots in a Compactor split into 18 and 9). A lane holding less than two operations' worth must not be split. Fill the 27 result slots: all lanes stop with "Output is full" and resume when space is freed. Starve the machine of power with several lanes active and check that no input is consumed without a result.
3. **Refusal and reject with hoppers.** With a hopper and with an Item Transfer, offer a crusher an item it cannot crush, an Electric Furnace an item that cannot be smelted, a combustion generator a non-fuel, a biomass generator coal, and an Alloy Smelter lane holding copper an ingredient that does not pair with copper. Each must stay in the hopper or source chest. Offer copper and tin from two hoppers to an Alloy Smelter and check that they pair up. Try the same items by hand and by shift-click. Then make an item unusable while it is inside (change a recipe with a datapack and reload): the machine must move it to the result slots, or show "Unsupported item" if the results are full. Confirm a hopper cannot place the Survey Miner's filter.
4. **Dismantle and re-place.** Charge a Mk III or higher machine and put items in it. Crouch-use the wrench: the machine must go to the player's inventory (or drop at the block if the inventory is full), its contents must drop exactly once, and the item must show its tier and stored energy. Place it again and check tier and energy. Break the same machine with a pickaxe instead: the dropped item must also keep tier and energy, and the inventory drops separately. Check that a player who is not the owner cannot dismantle a Survey Miner, and that dismantling is refused where block breaking is protected. Plain wrench use must still rotate without dropping anything.
5. **Conduit materials.** Check the item tooltips: 200, 1,000 and 5,000 FE/t per supplier for copper, gold and resonite. With one Mk VIII cell (25,600 FE/t) feeding consumers through each material in turn, measure delivery at 200, 1,000 and 5,000 FE/t. Mix one copper segment into a resonite network: the whole network must fall to 200 FE/t. Put a consumer directly beside the supplier while the supplier also feeds a copper network: the adjacent block must not be limited to 200 FE/t. Confirm that one supplier's total per tick never exceeds its tier's rate across direct neighbors, conduits and another mod's cable together.
6. **Sieve line.** Craft an Auto Sieve. Sieve gravel, sand and coarse dirt: each cycle takes 80 ticks at 15 FE/t at Mk I and gives one iron, copper or lead fragment. Over at least 200 cycles each, the second output should appear about 35% (tin fragment from gravel), 10% (gold fragment from sand) and 20% (flint from coarse dirt) of the time. Crush four matching fragments into one dust for all five metals; three fragments must not crush. Crush cobblestone into gravel and gravel into sand. Build the loop cobblestone → crusher → sieve → crusher → furnace and check that gravel routed to the centrifuge still gives flint.
7. **Bloom boost.** Craft a Resonance Bloom from a Resonite Crystal, any small flower and glowstone dust; try several small flowers. Plant it on soil and check that it cannot be placed on stone. Place one to six blooms within two blocks of a processing machine: the energy-bar tooltip must show +8% per bloom and stop at +40% with five. Check a bloom three blocks away in any direction does not count, and that one two blocks above or below does. Measure cycle time with and without blooms; power per tick rises with speed while energy per operation stays the same. Remove the blooms and confirm the boost drops within a few seconds. Blooms must not affect generators, cells or the miner.
8. **Progress page.** Open the Field Guide and select Progress. The page lists only the advancements the game has revealed to the player, so before the first milestone is earned it may show only its placeholder line. Craft a Machine Frame and reopen it: an earned milestone shows DONE, a milestone whose parent is done shows NEXT, a deeper one shows LOCKED, and more cards appear as the player advances. The footer shows done and revealed counts. Compare with the vanilla Advancements screen for all 25 milestones. Check scrolling, small GUI scales, rejoining the world, and that the page does nothing unexpected for a second player.
9. **Sounds and particles.** Run each of the nine processors and both fuel generators: a working machine shows its particles and plays its working sound at a low volume. A working Survey Miner shows particles and has no working sound; solar generators, cells, cores and terminals have neither. Add `"machineSounds": false` to `config/technologia.json`, restart, and confirm the working sounds stop while particles remain. Check block place/break sounds: metal for machines, cables, conduits and building blocks, glass for reinforced glass. Check that many active machines do not flood the sound engine.
10. **Alpha.3 world upgrade.** Back up an alpha.3 world that contains every machine type with items in inputs and outputs, charged cells, a miner with a filter, and a four-core Nexus. Load it in alpha.4. Every machine must load as Mk I with its energy; ingredients stay in the ingredient slots; results appear in the new result slots; the miner keeps its filter and owner; cores keep their 54 slots. Count items before and after. There must be no missing-registry warnings. An unfinished cycle restarts without consuming another input. Save, reload and check again.
11. **Akashic Tome on NeoForge.** Install the NeoForge 1.21.1 build of Akashic Tome with Technologia. With Akashic Tome's default settings, try to add the Field Guide to the tome, select it and use it, then detach it and check that the guide item is unchanged. Expected: the tome accepts the guide, because the guide's item id contains "guide". That expectation was read from Akashic Tome's source and has not been tested in game, so record what actually happens, including whether the Technologia guide opens from the tome. No Forge or Fabric build of Akashic Tome for 1.21.1 was found to test.
12. **Fabric and Forge first launch.** The automated tests for these loaders run on a headless server only, so this case is the first client check. On each loader, start a client and a dedicated server with only Technologia (plus Fabric API on Fabric). Check the log for errors, that all 40 blocks and 37 standalone items are present and textured, that the machine screen, guide and Progress page open, and that one tier kit, one dismantle and one conduit transfer work. Energy uses FE on Forge and Team Reborn Energy on Fabric.

## Survival workshop and recipes

1. In a fresh survival world, locate tin, lead, silver, nickel and resonite at their configured heights, including negative Y. Test fortune, silk touch and wrong-tier tools. Obtain the first circuit, frame and generator through recipes.
2. Power a crusher beside the combustion generator. Check one raw metal → two dust for iron, gold, copper, tin, lead, silver and nickel; smelt the dust to ingots and confirm tin and lead dust give the same smelting experience as the other dusts. Confirm coal → coal dust and reject charcoal for that processing recipe.
3. Craft an Alloy Smelter. Three copper plus one tin must yield four bronze; one iron plus two coal dust must yield one steel. Test both slot orders, insufficient quantities, larger stacks and unrelated items (which are now refused at insertion). Inputs are consumed only when a complete cycle finishes.
4. Press iron, copper, gold, bronze, steel, invar and electrum ingots into matching plates. Follow the recipes from bronze into conduits and solar, and from steel into the compactor, miner and advanced power. Check that no recipe depends on its own unobtainable output, and that each of the 17 building blocks has its own recipe.
5. Process all ten wood families, including stripped logs/wood and crimson/warped stems. Each sawmill cycle must produce six matching planks plus one sawdust. Fill all available space for the byproduct: the machine must stop without consuming input or spending more power. Clear space and resume.
6. Check compactor recipes for bulk metals, coal, redstone, lapis, diamonds, emeralds, sandstone, clay, Compressed Cobblestone and Compressed Gravel. Check centrifuge recipes: gravel → flint, clay → four clay balls, two moss → bone meal, four cactus → slime.
7. Recycle iron/gold tools and armor into three matching nuggets. Test damaged, named and enchanted items in a disposable test world; the whole item and its components are intentionally consumed. Other equipment must be refused.
8. Pause, remove input, replace it with another item/component variant and reload recipes mid-process. Progress must reset for a changed recipe/input identity, but survive adding more identical input. Change datapack duration, energy, output, byproduct, byproduct chance and a sieve recipe's mesh; confirm the next cycle uses the loaded recipe.
9. Fill outputs, then free capacity. Test partial stacks as well as empty slots. Break a machine while running, unload its chunk and restart the server: inventory drops exactly once on break; saved inventory, fuel, energy, tier and valid processing progress survive reload. A broken machine item keeps its tier and stored energy.

## Power and conduits

1. Check default combustion output at 40 FE/t and biomass at 20 FE/t at Mk I. Test biomass fuel durations for sawdust, saplings, wheat, kelp and sugar cane; coal must not work in the biomass generator. Confirm a generator stops burning fuel while its buffer cannot take a full tick of output, and that no fuel is consumed in that state.
2. Check solar output at 16 FE/t and advanced solar at 64 FE/t at Mk I. Test day/night transitions, rain, a solid roof and a dimension without skylight. Removing the obstruction or restoring clear daylight should resume generation.
3. Route power through straight lines, branches and loops, then mix conduit endpoints with direct neighbors. A Mk I Technologia supplier must spend at most 200 FE/t across all recipients, including multiple faces of the same recipient. Check total energy before/after simulated and actual transfer.
4. Charge both the one-million-FE and five-million-FE cells (Mk I capacities). Generators and Wireless Energy Receivers may charge cells; cells must not charge one another through adjacency or conduit loops. A paused generator may still export its stored energy.
5. Disconnect branches and unload adjacent chunks. Routing must remain bounded to 128 conduits without forcing chunks to load; a longer network shows "Conduit route too long" on the supplier. Add and remove a conduit or an endpoint and confirm delivery follows the change at once. Test a continuously hungry endpoint alongside another consumer for starvation.
6. On each loader, connect representative external sources and sinks directly to machine energy adapters, then test conduit delivery from a Technologia source to an external sink. Conduits have no general external input buffer. Check FE simulation and cancelled/nested Fabric transactions.

## Nexus and item automation

1. Connect one, two and four cores to a terminal. Capacity must show 54, 108 and 216 slots respectively. Place items in the last slot of the fourth core and withdraw them through the terminal. Opening a core directly must show only that core's inventory.
2. Use two players and hoppers concurrently. Exercise stack/one/shift withdrawals, cursor deposits, full player inventories and repeated fast clicks while another viewer changes sorting or contents. Total item count must remain constant; named/component variants stay distinct. The catalogue must update when contents, search text or sort order change.
3. Add, replace or remove a core with the terminal open, then reopen it. Old actions must be rejected and the new view must reflect the current capacity. Break cable/core/terminal, move out of range or unload a connection. No stale access is allowed. Five connected cores or more than 128 network blocks must fail closed without moving or deleting their contents.
4. Place Item Transfers between chests and machines in all six directions. Check rear extraction and arrow-side insertion, at most eight items per eight ticks. Put a transfer on the side and on the top of a vanilla furnace: it must take smelted items and leave fuel and ingredient. Feed a stack of books into a chiseled bookshelf. Test double chests, partially full slots and a completely full target; nothing may disappear or remain in an invisible transfer buffer. Check that the selection outline matches the model in every direction.
5. Set a filter with a held sample, confirm the sample count is unchanged, and clear it with an empty hand. Test exact component matching with named items, then save/reload the filter. Apply redstone to pause transfer and remove it to resume.
6. Feed both alloy inputs with filtered transfers or hoppers and extract only outputs. Automatic insertion must not enter outputs; automatic extraction must not pull processor inputs or the miner's retained filter. Test separate input/output layouts after reopening each machine screen.
7. Use the wrench to rotate stocked machines, Item Transfers, Vector Plates, control panels, ventilation grilles, Metal Shelves and Tool Cabinets; inventories, energy and filters must remain intact. Crouch-use on a machine dismantles it (see the alpha.4 section). Verify miner ownership still applies.
8. Exercise representative external pipes and Create funnels/chutes on supported versions. Item Transfer and the Eject button currently support vanilla `Container` and sided-container inventories, not capability-only third-party inventories. A kinetic bridge is not implemented.
